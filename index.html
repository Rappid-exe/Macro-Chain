The Mathematics

Shannon Entropy

Shannon Entropy quantifies the information content of a signal. Macro-Chain applies it to measure how rapidly a market event's novelty decays across participants, identifying the point at which a causal link is already priced in.

Bayesian Causal Networks

Bayesian Causal Networks model probabilistic dependencies between economic variables. The system uses directed acyclic graphs to validate whether an observed correlation reflects a genuine causal pathway or a spurious coincidence.
